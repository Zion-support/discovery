# Discovery — delivery flow & network links

## Instant results delivery (live)
When a visitor submits the questionnaire at https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/:
1. Results are POSTed via FormSubmit AJAX to **commercial@ziontechgroup.com** (subject includes name, company, and score).
2. The client is CC'd (`_cc`) and receives an **autoresponse** confirming the commercial team got a copy and linking to recommended apps at https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/.
3. No backend required — always online and free.
